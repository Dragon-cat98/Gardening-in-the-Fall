A cozy gardening game where you plant and harvest pumpkins


play at: https://dragoncat420.itch.io/gardening-in-the-fall

# How to play

**CONTROLS**:
* Mouse movement to move
* left mouse click to click

**Game loop**
+ **Plant** the seeds
+ **Harvest** the plants
+ **Sell** the crops
+ **Remove** the roots
+ **Buy** more seeds
+ **Repeat**
